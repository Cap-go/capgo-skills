Captured from the live marketing site on **2026-10-08** from https://capgo.app/pricing/

Always re-check the pricing page before quoting numbers to customers. This file is a point-in-time snapshot for agents.

## Trial

- **14-day unlimited free trial**
- **No credit card** required to start (also stated on the homepage)

## Billing periods

Each paid plan shows:

- **Yearly billing** prices (examples below labeled "per month" when billed annually)
- **Monthly billing** prices (higher list prices on the comparison table)

Yearly checkout shows about **20% savings** versus paying monthly (pricing calculator label: "Yearly Plan -20%").

## Credit-based overages (pay as you go)

Available on all plans and as **Credits only (no plan)** at **$0/month** base.

Capgo states:

- Credits are **prepaid**, valid **1 year**
- Usage beyond included limits can use credits
- **No automatic overage billing without your confirmation**
- Email warnings at **50%, 70%, 90%, and 100%** of limits; at the limit, **new update downloads pause** while apps keep the current version
